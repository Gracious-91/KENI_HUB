ALL 'BOUT KENI.

Where Logic Meets Expression.

Who Is Keni?

I'm Keni — a curious mind, a creative spirit, and someone who is always becoming. I'm drawn to both structure and expression: the logic behind technology and the freedom of movement, art, and creativity. I'm learning to embrace all the different sides of me instead of trying to fit myself into just one box.

What I Love

I love movement, creativity, and the little things that make life feel alive. I love dancing, skating, creating, exploring, and being surrounded by nature. Give me a quiet forest, the sound of flowing water, birds in the trees, a breeze on my skin, and space to simply exist — and I'm happy. I love making things with my hands,my mind and my body, discovering new ideas, and finding different ways to express myself.

What Moves Me

I'm moved by growth, expression, connection, and the possibility of becoming more than I was yesterday. I'm fascinated by the moment an idea starts living outside your head — when a thought becomes code, a feeling becomes movement, or an imagination becomes something real. I want to keep learning, creating, and leaving little pieces of meaning wherever I go. Because for me, life is about becoming — and finding my own way to express it.
